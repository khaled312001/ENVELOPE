#### Rules stated inside definitions

<a id="b21-gate"></a>
#### B.2.1 Gate level ¶2
- **Pages:** EN p26 · AR p26
- **EN:** "The gate level of a plot shall be a minimum height of +300 mm from the edge of the road or a slope of between 2-5% from the edge of the road to the plot’s access point."
- **AR:** "يجب أن يكون المنسوب المرجعي لقطعة الأرض على ارتفاع +300 mm كحدٍ أدنى من حافة الطريق أو منحدر بين 2-5% من حافة الطريق إلى نقطة الوصول إلى قطعة الأرض."
- **Values:** gate level `≥ +300 mm` above road edge, **or** slope `2 %–5 %` from road edge to the plot access point
- **Conditions / exceptions:** Two alternatives joined by "or". The road level may be existing or future (from the first paragraph of the definition). Printed as its own paragraph inside a definition, but it is a mandatory requirement.
- **Refs:** — (gate level is the datum for "Basement floor" and "Ground floor", EN p23, p26)
- **Obligation:** shall
- **Mechanisable from plot geometry + inputs?** partly — the test is arithmetic, but road edge level and access-point level are survey inputs an affection plan does not carry.

<a id="b21-mech"></a>
#### B.2.1 Mechanical parking ¶2
- **Pages:** EN p28 · AR p28
- **EN:** "Public occupancy is prohibited above street level."
- **AR:** "ويُحظر الإشغال العام فوق مستوى الشارع."
- **Values:** —
- **Conditions / exceptions:** Applies to mechanical parking (parking areas employing machines, lifts, elevators or other mechanical devices moving vehicles from and to street level). Printed as the last sentence of the definition.
- **Refs:** —
- **Obligation:** shall (prohibition)
- **Mechanisable from plot geometry + inputs?** partly — once mechanical parking is declared, any public-occupancy use on its levels above street level is a schedule check; the choice of mechanical parking is not.
