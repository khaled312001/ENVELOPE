THE TRUS T L AY ER

Speed is worthless if the number cannot be defended

Four mechanisms, all built in Phase 0, because none of them can be retrofitted later.

Provenance            Every number resolves to a cited rule, a declared assumption, an objective, or a named person. The system never
                      says "the AI decided."

Human-approved rules  AI may draft a rule. A licensed architect approves it. Unapproved rules cannot be loaded by the engine -- there is
                      no override flag.

Assumption register   Where no rule governs, the system declares an assumption, ranks it by how much it moves the answer, and
                      requires acknowledgement before export.

Invariant checks      Independent arithmetic proofs: areas must sum, ratios must reconcile, parking must fit. A configuration that fails is
                      never emitted.

No hidden defaults. If we filled a gap, it is amber on the screen and listed in the report.

THE CAPACITY MODEL

Five capacities. Most feasibility work reports one.

Knowing which limit binds is worth more than knowing the maximum -- it tells you whether to negotiate, redesign, or accept.

Regulatory capacity              What FAR and the area caps permit                                                           Phase 0

Geometric capacity               What the envelope physically holds within height and footprint limits                       Phase 0

Parking capacity                 What the achievable parking supply can actually support                                     Phase 0

Commercially preferred capacity  What the developer's own declared objectives select                                         Phase 1

Historically observed capacity   What comparable approved plots actually achieved                                            Phase 2

The governing capacity is the smallest of them -- and we name it, rather than reporting a maximum the plot will never deliver.

THE AI BOUNDARY

AI reads. The engine computes. Humans approve.

Language models are excellent at reading documents and writing explanations, and unacceptable at computing an area. We use each for
what it is reliable at.
