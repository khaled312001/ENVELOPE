# Ramp — the rules, as Refero records them

Source: <https://styles.refero.design/style/b38702a0-75ab-474c-9106-00b624535825>, read 2026-09-22.

**Register.** "Editorial finance desk, black-and-white pages with a single neon
highlighter." A warm off-white canvas `#f4f2f0`, white cards, ink `#0c0a08`, and one
chromatic accent — a highlighter yellow `#e4f222` — "reserved exclusively for actions and
CTAs".

**Elevation.** "1px hairlines replace shadows" — a white card on the canvas with a `#e5e7eb`
edge; box-shadow is not applied to cards, modals or panels.

**Type.** One family at one weight (400); "size and tracking drive hierarchy; no bold or
semibold variants exist". Display lines at line height ≤ 1.05.

**Layout.** "Consistently left-aligned within centered containers; never centered
body/headlines." Stacked metric strips; a full-width dark band for live figures.

**Shape.** 6px buttons and tags, 10px inputs, 12–16px cards; "never fully rounded pills".

**Motion.** 0.3–0.4s, ease-out, on colour, background, border, fill and stroke only — "no
transform or opacity theatrics. Motion signals state change only."
