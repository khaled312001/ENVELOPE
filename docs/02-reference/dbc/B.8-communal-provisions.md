<a id="layout-notes"></a>
## Notes for a ground-floor / parking-level layout engine

What B.8 does and does not give a car-park / ground-floor layout:

- **Waste room location (vertical):** ground floor preferred; B1 permitted only, never B2 or lower — B.8.4 ¶4 b) (EN p113) and B.8.5.2.2 b) (EN p115). B1 adds 1 h fire rating, 10 ACH dedicated extract (B.8.4 ¶4 a), c)) and a service elevator; if collection happens on B1 the level must take the collection vehicle (no dimensions given).
- **Waste room location (plan):** inside the plot boundary, easily accessible, unimpeded collection-vehicle access, away from development ingress/egress "where possible" — B.8.5.2.2 a) (EN p115). Near the access roads when the chute is away from them — B.8.5.7 ¶5 (EN p119). A 4.5 m³ container only if the room directly overlooks the street — Table B.29 NOTE (EN p117).
- **Collection vehicle:** B.8 gives **no** vehicle dimensions, turning radius, headroom or loading-bay size. Only route rules: access route ≥1.2 m (should), gradient ≤8.33 % (1:12), drop kerbs at roadways, manual haul ≤7 m for ≤1,000 l and ≤50 m for ≤360 l, none for >1,500 l — B.8.5.2.2 c) (EN p115). Compactor footprint must be accounted for in "service bay(s) as well as the site access" — B.8.5.6 ¶3 (EN p118).
- **Waste room size:** GA 230–460 m² → 1.2 m × 1.8 m on ground floor only (B.8.5.2.3 ¶5 a), EN p116). GA > 460 m² → sized from generation: residential 12 kg/100 m² NA, commercial 12, office 5, hotel 3 kg/room + 5 kg/suite (¶3, EN p116), ×2 days minimum (¶1), mapped to containers by Table B.29 (EN p117), packed with 150 mm between containers/walls and 600 mm to the door wall, door ≥1.2 m, clear height ≥2.4 m (¶5 d) 1)–4), EN p116). Recommended +5 m² sorting (B.8.5.3, EN p118) and +5 m² bulky/special (B.8.5.4, EN p118). Container footprints: Tables B.27/B.28 (EN p117); compactors Table B.30 (EN p118). Separate onsite store (outside the building) only when the largest storey GA < 250 m², ≥2.5 m horizontal / 5 m vertical from openable living-space windows, not impeding access points, roofed (B.8.5.2.1, EN p114).
- **Refuse chute trigger:** residential, height > G+3 and any storey > 250 m² (B.8.5.7 ¶1, EN p119); chute ≥600 mm diameter; 900 × 1,000 mm access area per floor; door ≥915 mm. Every such tower therefore lands a chute in a ground/B1 collection room.
- **Electrical rooms:** transformer, LV switchgear, HV and generator rooms are preferred at ground level, allowed in basements only per G.7.6 + UAE FLSC Table 1.9; **RMU rooms are not allowed in basements** — B.8.4 ¶2–¶3 (EN p113). No sizes in B.8 (see Part G).
- **Guard rooms, meter rooms, janitor rooms, substations:** **not addressed in B.8.** The only service room sized here is the cleaner’s room: ≥1.5 m² in any building > 100 m² GA (B.8.1.6, EN p104).
- **Amenity ratios for residential:** **none in B.8.** Gyms and pools are regulated only "when provided" (B.8.3.1, B.8.3.2); open spaces only refer to planting/shading in B.10.5 (B.8.3.4, EN p113). Prayer rooms are required for public, business and hotel buildings and labour/staff accommodation, not for ordinary residential (B.8.3.3 ¶1, EN p112). Pool fence ≥1,200 mm from plot limits (B.8.3.2.3 ¶3, EN p108) matters only if a pool is placed at grade.
